How to Use

To use the Home Mortgage Calculator, enter the amount of the mortgage, the interest rate of the loan (as a percentage), and the number of years. Then click on the "Calculate" button to see an estimate of your monthly payment.

Note: Do not use dollar signs or commas when entering the loan amount.
